Style Heaven B2B Sourcing

Join as a business partner

Style Heaven sources fashion through vetted business partners — manufacturers, wholesalers, brands and established suppliers — and sells directly to consumers on styleheaven.example.com. We are not a reseller marketplace: this page is for businesses who want to supply Style Heaven, not for customers.

How the sourcing relationship works

  1. Style Heaven purchases stock or agrees supply terms directly with the partner business.
  2. Style Heaven lists the resulting products on its own site and sells them to individual customers (B2C).
  3. Provide accurate business, contact and GST information at enquiry stage.
  4. Only propose products and images you are authorised to supply.
  5. Product quality, descriptions and stock commitments must be accurate.
  6. Supply must be fulfilled within the agreed commercial terms.
  7. Pricing, minimum volumes and settlement follow the signed partner agreement.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Your business and contact details
  • GST registration information, where applicable
  • Product categories you can supply
  • Available monthly supply volume
  • Locations and fulfilment capability
  • Any commercial or catalogue questions
Business enquiries are handled separately from customer support.

Our sourcing team reviews your requirement and comes back with next steps for verification, catalogue fit, pricing and a supply agreement.
