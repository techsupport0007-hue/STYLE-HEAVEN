# Style Heaven — Merchant Onboarding & Operating Rules

## Who can apply
- Fashion retailers
- Boutiques
- Wholesalers
- Online sellers
- Resellers
- Brands/manufacturers with an appropriate commercial arrangement

## Information requested
- Legal/business name
- Contact person
- Business email and mobile
- GSTIN where registered/applicable
- Business type
- City/state/PIN
- Product categories
- Expected monthly order volume
- Sourcing and fulfilment requirements

## Verification principles
1. Business information must be accurate.
2. Products must be genuine and lawfully supplied.
3. Merchant must have rights/permission for catalogue images and product content.
4. Counterfeit, prohibited, misleading or unlawfully supplied goods are not permitted.
5. Prices, descriptions, sizes, fabric information and fulfilment commitments must be kept accurate.
6. Merchant must follow agreed order, support, return/claim and settlement procedures.
7. Style Heaven may request additional documents before activation or during periodic review.
8. Accounts may be suspended while verification, quality, compliance or transaction concerns are investigated.

## Approval flow
Application → Business verification → Category/commercial review → Merchant agreement → Account activation → B2B access

## Operating model
After approval, the merchant relationship can cover catalogue sourcing, business orders, fulfilment coordination, support and commercial settlement according to the final agreement. The website currently presents the workflow and onboarding layer; production settlement and merchant accounting should be connected to the final commercial model before launch.
